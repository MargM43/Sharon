Shazza's Dog Grooming

Shazza’s Dog Grooming is owned and operated by Sharon Smith.

Sharon has 12 years experience in the dog grooming industry. She is a fully qualified professional dog groomer, with formal qualifications from the Career Academy.

Through them, Sharon has obtained four certificates and a Diploma in dog grooming.

Her dog grooming facility is fully set up for all of the services she offers her many happy customers.

To find out more about Sharon and her qualifications, go to her About Mepage.
